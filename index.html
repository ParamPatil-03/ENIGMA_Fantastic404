HOW TOUCHSTONE WORKS

01

Submit the Claim

The buyer or seller submits the declared material specification, transaction volume, and basic logistical details for the pending trade.

02

Document Cross-Check

Touchstone compares the declared spec against the seller's filed regulatory profile (CTO, Form-4) and produces a Verification Confidence Score within hours.

03

Physical Assay if Flagged

If the confidence score falls below threshold, or the client explicitly requests it, a Touchstone field team is dispatched to independently test the actual material on-site.

04

Certified Report + Support

The client receives a verification certificate. If any required legal documentation (Rule 9, manifests) is missing or expired, we separately offer to help obtain it.

The Taloja Precedent

In a prominent matter before the National Green Tribunal involving manufacturers in the Taloja MIDC cluster, industrial chemical by-products were transferred onward while a core regulatory question remained unanswered: did the material's actual characteristics match what the generator had formally declared in their environmental consents?

The dispute highlighted a systemic vulnerability across industrial corridors — registered facility filings often drift severely from daily operational reality, leaving downstream buyers legally exposed.
